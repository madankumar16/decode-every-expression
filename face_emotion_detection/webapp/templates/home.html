How it works

Seven expressions

Happy, sad, surprise, anger, fear, disgust and neutral, each with a confidence score.

Live results

Frames are analysed as you watch, about five per second.

Nothing kept by default

Frames are processed in memory and discarded. Snapshots are saved only when you take one.

An estimate, not a verdict

Expressions don't always match feelings. Don't use results for hiring, medical or legal decisions.
