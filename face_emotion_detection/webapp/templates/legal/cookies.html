Cookie Policy

Last updated {{ site.policy_date }}

We use a single, strictly necessary cookie. Without it you couldn't stay signed in.

The cookie

  • Name: es_session
  • Purpose: keeps you signed in and protects forms from forgery.
  • Lasts: up to 12 hours, or until you sign out.
  • Set by: us. It is never shared.

What we don't use

No analytics, advertising, social media or third-party cookies. Because the only cookie is essential, there's nothing to opt in to.

Local storage

Your browser remembers that you closed the cookie notice. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.
